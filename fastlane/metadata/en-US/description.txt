Guide a hen across grassy fields, busy roads, rivers, and railroad tracks. Tap to hop forward, or swipe and use the direction buttons to move sideways or back. Dodge cars and trains, watch for railroad signals, and ride moving logs across rivers. Pick up coins along the way and see how far you can get.

Start a free run or replay the same daily route until local midnight. The Daily tab tracks your progress toward distance, coin, and run goals. Choose a meadow, orchard, harvest, or frost landscape for a free run.

Spend earned coins to unlock hen colors in Flock. Every hen plays the same way. In Records, see your best distances and the current week’s top runs, share a scorecard, or paste one from another player.
