Guide a hen across grassy fields, busy roads, rivers and railway tracks. Tap to hop forwards, or swipe and use the direction buttons to move sideways or back. Watch for cars and trains, look out for railway signals and ride moving logs across rivers. Collect coins as you go and see how far you can get.

Start a free run or replay the same daily route until local midnight. The Daily tab tracks your progress towards distance, coin and run goals. Choose a meadow, orchard, harvest or frost landscape for a free run.

Spend earned coins to unlock hen colours in Flock. Every hen plays the same way. In Records, see your best distances and the current week’s top runs, share a scorecard or paste one from another player.
