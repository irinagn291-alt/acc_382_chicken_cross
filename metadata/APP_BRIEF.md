# Chicken Cross

## What it is

Chicken Cross is an offline arcade game for children and casual players. Guide a hen through traffic, across rivers and over railway tracks, collect coins for new looks, and try to beat your own distance on free runs or the daily route.

## Launch and onboarding

1. A plain cream launch screen appears briefly.
2. The app opens directly on “Play”, with “OFFLINE ARCADE” above “Chicken Cross”. The bottom tabs are “Play”, “Flock”, “Daily”, and “Records”. The board shows the current landscape and the selected hen. On a fresh install, “Steps”, “Best”, and “Coins” all read zero.
3. The standard start card reads “Ready, steady,  
   cross.” followed by “A little hen. A wide world.  
   How far will you hop?” Tap “Let’s cross” for a free run or “Try today’s route” for the daily route. Below the board, the instruction reads “Tap the field to hop · Swipe to steer”.
4. When the board is short, the compact card reads “Chicken Cross” and “Tap the field to hop · Swipe to steer”, with “Let’s cross” as its only button. The daily route is still available from “Daily”.

There is no separate onboarding, account setup, or permission prompt at launch.

### “Records” — “Your records”

## Features

- “Free run” with a fresh route each time, four-way hopping, moving cars, logs, trains, railway signals, trees, and collectible coins.
- “Daily route” with the same course for repeat attempts on a given day and three daily progress goals.
- “Seasonal places” with four always-open landscapes and the “Follow the season” option for free runs.
- “Your little flock” with six cosmetic hen looks, unlocked using coins found during play.
- “Your records” with all-time and daily bests, weekly run history, and directly exchanged “Scorecards”.
- “Gentle haptics”, optional “Daily reminders”, and reduced hen animation when iOS Reduce Motion is enabled.
- Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, plus “Erase local progress”.

## Behaviours that can look like bugs

- The direction arrows are disabled until a run is active. Tap “Let’s cross”, start a daily route, or tap “Keep hopping” as appropriate. Board edges, trees, and the five-row backtracking limit can block a move. Very rapid repeat inputs can be ignored.
- Leaving “Play”, opening “Settings”, or switching away from the app pauses the run. Return to “Play” and tap “Keep hopping”. Starting a route from “Daily” while another run is paused saves the old run as finished and starts the new one.
- “Hop again” keeps the previous run type. Daily attempts repeat the same course until the local date changes. To switch to a free run after a daily run, use an “Explore this place” card under “Seasonal places”.
- A run still active or paused when the app detects a new local date ends with “A new daily route is ready. Your last route is saved.” Start another run to continue. Daily totals reset with the date, and “This week’s best runs” uses the current calendar week.
- “Collect” stays disabled until the coin balance reaches the cost on that hen’s card. Earn more coins during runs. Coins are credited as they are picked up, and the same coin cannot be picked up twice within one run.
- “This week’s best runs” can be empty even while “All time” shows a score. Finish a run in the current week to populate it. The weekly list displays the best 12, while each scorecard ranking displays the best ten.
- “Add scorecard” is disabled while “Paste a scorecard” is empty. “Invalid scorecard” appears for rejected input, including your own current-format card. Paste a valid card from another player and tap “Add scorecard” again. Updated cards from the same player replace the earlier entry and can retain a higher score already received. Past-day scores disappear from the “☀︎” ranking.
- In “For grown-ups”, a blank or incorrect answer followed by “Continue” shows “Give it another try.” Enter `15` and tap “Continue”, or tap “Cancel”. After allowing notifications in iOS settings, return to the app and turn on “Remind me each day” if it is still off.

## Starter content and resume

“Little Ivory” is owned and selected from the start. All four landscapes and the current “Daily route” are immediately playable. Coins and scores begin at zero, with no sample run history or imported scorecards.

An unfinished run can be resumed with “Keep hopping” while the app’s session is still alive and the local date has not changed. After a full relaunch, “Play” opens ready for a new run. Coins collected and best distances recorded during the unfinished run persist, while the unfinished run itself does not become a completed history entry.

## Permissions

Notifications are the only permission requested. iOS asks for permission to show alerts and play sounds when the player turns on “Remind me each day” and has yet to grant permission. The app supplies no custom usage-description string for this system prompt.

## Absent

Login and accounts, in-app purchases, ads, analytics, an account deletion flow, and an App Tracking Transparency prompt are absent. There is no free-form user-generated content or public posting. Players can exchange game-generated scorecards as text.

## Data and support

Game progress, preferences, and imported scorecards are stored on the device. Widgets read the same local progress. A scorecard leaves the device when the player chooses to send it through the iOS share sheet. There is no automatic score sync and no in-app “Contact” or “Support” control.

## Scanning and health

None.

## Platform

- iPhone and iPad require iOS or iPadOS 18.2 or later.
- iPhone supports portrait and landscape in both directions. iPad also supports upside-down portrait. A wide, short play layout places the board and controls side by side.
- The native UI is localized in English, German, Brazilian Portuguese, and French. The quoted app labels in this brief are the English versions. The home-screen name is “Chicken Cross” in all four localizations.
- Gameplay has no region gate. Daily turnover follows the device’s local date, weekly records follow its calendar week, and reminders use local time. “Follow the season” changes landscapes by calendar month on a fixed schedule.
